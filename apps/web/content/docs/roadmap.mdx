Jev Events is young. The core API is settling, but expect small breaking changes until 1.0; each one is listed in the changelog.

## Available

- **Core**: `listen()`, typed events, policies and review bands, dry-run, protected users, budgets, rate limits, stale dropping, the text cache, `burst()`, `logTo()` and `mockJev()`.
- **Sources**: `from()`, `webhook()`, and the read-only public Twitch chat and Bluesky firehose.
- **Twitch**: anonymous and signed-in chat, `timeout`, `ban`, `deleteMessage`, `warn`, `reply`, `say` and `clip`, and device-code sign-in.
- **CLI**: `watch` and `auth twitch`.
- **Benchmarks**: a labeled chat dataset and an open runner.

## In development

In this order:

1. **Discord**: messages from your server through the Gateway, with `deleteMessage`, `timeout`, `ban`, `kick`, `react`, `reply` and forwarding to a mod-log channel.
2. **YouTube**: live chat, and comments under your videos. Live chat actions are delete, ban and reply; comment actions are hold for review, reject, publish, delete and reply. A quota manager keeps you inside YouTube's daily API quota and saves room for actions.
3. **Gmail and Google Calendar**: new mail with label, archive, star, mark-read and trash; new invitations and upcoming events with accept, decline and maybe. You'll bring your own Google Cloud project, so your mail never passes through anyone else's app.

Each connector ships with its own guide and example project.
